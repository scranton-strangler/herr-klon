Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Purpose

Rclone Backup is a private application used exclusively by its owner to manage files and perform backups between a personal computer and Google Drive using Rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authorize access to the owner's Google Drive account.

Access is used only to read, upload, download, modify and delete files as instructed by the owner through Rclone.

Data Collection and Storage

This website does not collect personal information, Google Drive files or authentication tokens.

Rclone operates on the owner's computer. Authentication credentials are stored locally, and file transfers are performed directly using Google's APIs.

Data Sharing

This application does not sell or disclose Google user data to third parties. Google Drive services are used solely to perform the operations requested by the owner.

Data Retention and Revocation

This website does not retain Google user data.

The owner can revoke access at any time through Google Account settings.
